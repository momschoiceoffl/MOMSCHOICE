Terms, Shipping & Returns

Please read our store policies carefully before making a purchase.

1. Shipping Policy

We ship across India through trusted courier partners.

  • Dispatch Time: Orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours (not exceeding 2 business days).
  • Delivery Time: Delivery usually takes up to 7 working days based on transportation. However, it may extend up to 15 days for remote locations or unforeseen transit delays.
  • Shipping Charges: Free for orders above ₹2000. Below ₹2000, a flat rate of ₹60–₹150 applies based on your pincode zone.
  • Tracking: A WhatsApp tracking link will be sent to you within 24 hours of dispatch.

2. Return, Exchange & Cancellation Policy

ALL SALES ARE FINAL. We strictly do not accept returns, exchanges, or cancellations under any circumstances.

  • No Cancellations: Orders cannot be cancelled once placed. Please double-check your cart before checking out.
  • No Returns or Exchanges: We do not accept returns or exchanges for size issues, change of mind, or colour variations.
  • Damage Claims: In the extremely rare case of a damaged or wrong item, you must record a clear parcel opening video and send it to us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery. Claims without a video will be rejected. If approved, we will offer a replacement (subject to stock availability) or store credit. No cash refunds will be provided.

3. Refund Policy

We do not offer cash refunds.

  • If an order is cancelled by us due to stock unavailability, a full refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.
  • Approved damage claims (with video proof) will be resolved via a replacement or store credit only.

4. Payment Policy (Manual WhatsApp Checkout)

We operate on a WhatsApp-first enquiry model to ensure personal service and order accuracy.

  • How it works:
    1. You send an enquiry via our WhatsApp button.
    2. We confirm product availability, final price, and shipping cost through a WhatsApp conversation.
    3. Once confirmed, we share our UPI ID or UPI QR code directly on WhatsApp.
    4. You complete the payment and send a screenshot of the payment confirmation back to us on WhatsApp.
    5. Order is processed only after we receive and verify your payment screenshot.
  • Accepted Payment Methods: UPI transfer (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or any UPI app) or direct bank transfer via the UPI ID shared on WhatsApp.
  • Not Available: We do NOT offer Cash on Delivery (COD). We do NOT accept credit cards, debit cards, net banking, or any third-party payment gateway. All payments are direct UPI transfers only.
  • Order Processing: Orders are processed only after we have received your payment screenshot and verified the transaction. We will send you a dispatch confirmation on WhatsApp.
  • Security: We never ask for OTPs, UPI PINs, or card details. If anyone claims to be us and asks for these, please report immediately.

5. Privacy Policy

  • We collect: Name, phone, delivery address, pincode, email (optional), order history, and payment screenshot references.
  • We use this for: Order fulfilment, delivery, customer support, and order status updates via WhatsApp.
  • We do NOT: Sell your data, share it with third-party marketers, or store your UPI PIN/bank credentials.
